# Blue Owl Capital

> Blue Owl Capital Inc. (NYSE: OWL) is a New York-headquartered alternative asset manager formed
> in 2021 from the merger of Owl Rock and Dyal Capital, with Oak Street joining in 2022. It manages
> over $319 billion of assets across three platforms — Credit (direct lending), Real Assets (net
> lease real estate and digital infrastructure) and GP Strategic Capital (minority stakes in
> alternative asset managers) — plus insurance solutions, and serves institutional investors,
> financial advisors, individual investors and insurance companies. Figures are as stated on
> its own site in September 2026.

## Machine-readable API surface

**Blue Owl Capital publishes no API.** As of 2026-09-19 the company operates no developer
program, no developer portal, no SDK, and no machine-readable contract of any kind. This was
established by probe, not by assumption:

- No OpenAPI, Swagger, GraphQL SDL, AsyncAPI, MCP or A2A surface on any host the company
  controls (`/openapi.json`, `/swagger.json`, `/api-docs`, `/docs`, `/graphql`,
  `/.well-known/agent-card.json`, `/.well-known/agent.json` and the rest all return a real
  Drupal 404 on `www.blueowl.com`).
- `developer.blueowl.com` and `api.blueowl.com` — the hostnames an earlier scaffold of this
  profile named — do not resolve at all (DNS NXDOMAIN). They were never real hosts.
- Every `/.well-known/` discovery path on `www.blueowl.com` and `wealth.blueowl.com` returns
  404; `ir.blueowl.com` (a Q4 investor-relations platform) answers a few of them with an
  11-byte "Invalid key" body, which is not a document (see
  `well-known/blue-owl-capital-well-known.yml` for the full probe record).
- The published sitemap (`https://www.blueowl.com/sitemap.xml`, 785 URLs) contains no
  developer, API, docs or integration page. Its sections are platforms (credit, real-assets,
  gp-strategic-capital, insurance-solutions), who-we-serve, about-us, our-team, insights,
  news, careers, contact and legal.
- `docs.blueowl.com` is a CNAME to CloudFiles (a document-sharing service) and redirects to
  cloudfiles.io; it is not a documentation site.
- No first-party package exists on npm or PyPI. A GitHub organization named "Blue Owl Capital"
  (`github.com/BlueOwlCapital`) holds a single fork of Apache Superset and carries no website,
  email or other evidence tying it to the company, so it is not recorded as the company's org.
- The only investor-facing software surfaces are gated third-party portals reached from
  `https://www.blueowl.com/portals`: the Blue Owl Investor Portal on iCapital
  (`blueowl.icapitalnetwork.com`) and DST Vision for advisor/individual access to its REITs and
  BDCs. Neither documents an API for Blue Owl.

If you are an agent looking for a callable Blue Owl Capital API, there is not one to call.
Public company data about Blue Owl is available from the SEC (EDGAR filings for NYSE: OWL) and
from the investor-relations site, not from a Blue Owl-operated API.

## Security and fraud

- [Fraud Awareness Notice](https://www.blueowl.com/fraud-awareness-notice): states that Blue
  Owl personnel email only from `@blueowl.com`, that the primary website is blueowl.com, and
  that the current lists of official product, IR and fund sites live on the Our Products and
  Investor Portal pages. This is NOT a vulnerability-disclosure program; no security.txt, bug
  bounty or trust center is published.
